# One minute changes
Mode: Operate. Help a learner and followers of smallpancake’s guitar practice diary complete a one-minute chord-change round, review its estimated count and save progress in their own browser.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Invite diary followers to practice along with the same tools, with the chord-change exercise immediately available.
OWN-WORLD: Offer both palettes through the shared Palette selector: default Pancake from the user’s blog, or Original pastel with purple actions, lavender prompts, mint guides and pink accents. The following colors describe Pancake. Preserve the established cream paper, warm brown actions, pale peach timer, honey log, peach encouragement, Fredoka headings and DM Sans controls. The exact public name is smallpancake’s guitar practice room.
STORY: Recognize the diary’s practice room, choose a tool or chord pair, check input or choose manual mode, practice for a minute and review before saving. No account is needed.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full two-line brand wordmark and shared tool navigation, existing One minute changes heading and sun illustration, a short welcome for diary followers, then the established practice-panel/sidebar layout.
FORM: Narrow branding and audience-copy extension. Keep the exercise, storage and visual world intact. The shared header invites visitors to Practice along with us; the footer connects the guitar diary to daily practice. Titles and sharing metadata carry the full brand. Do not invent social handles or profile links.
FINISH: Verify the full wordmark and welcome fit desktop and mobile while preserving readable practice controls and keyboard access.
